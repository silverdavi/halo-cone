CERTIFICATE FOR THE ATTACHED 8B TABLE
===================================

Run: python certify.py
Requires Python 3 standard library only. No packages or network access.
The script writes certificate.json and asserts all claimed signs and bounds.
An optional command-line argument selects the input CSV, but its SHA256 must
match the supplied table. The certificate is tied to that exact numerical input.

THEOREM: UNIMODALITY SUFFICES

Suppose phi is continuous on [0,U], phi(0)=0, phi>0 on (0,U), locally
absolutely continuous, strictly increasing up to c, and nonincreasing after c.
Define h(q)=q integral_q^U phi(u)/u^2 du. Then h' has exactly one zero q* in
(0,c), and h'(q)>0 below q*, h'(q)<0 above q* up to U.

Proof. Differentiation gives
  h'=integral_q^U phi(u)/u^2 du - phi(q)/q,
  h''=-phi'(q)/q almost everywhere.
Thus h' is strictly decreasing on (0,c). Continuity at zero gives h(0+)=0:
split the integral at epsilon, bounding the first part by
sup_{[0,epsilon]} phi and the other part by O(q). Since h>0 inside, h' must
be positive somewhere before c, and hence everywhere sufficiently near zero.
For q>=c,
  integral_q^U phi(u)/u^2 du <= phi(q)(1/q-1/U),
so h'(q)<=-phi(q)/U<0. Continuity of h' and strict decrease before c prove
existence, uniqueness, and the sign statement.

The supplied table satisfies this hypothesis by exact rational comparisons
of every consecutive ordinate: slopes are strictly positive before the first
maximum and nonpositive afterward. The mode positions are 6.44, 6.34, 6.56.
The proof therefore covers every q, not merely a numerical grid.

Strict log-concavity, positivity inside, and vanishing endpoint values are a
sufficient stronger hypothesis. For example u^alpha(U-u)^beta with alpha,beta>0
is strictly log-concave. A product of positive log-concave factors preserves
log-concavity. An actual beta-decay shape with Coulomb corrections or a mixture
of final states must be checked; its physical name alone supplies no such proof.

ELEMENTARY INTEGRALS AND RIGOROUS LOG BOUNDS

On a segment [a,b], phi(u)=s*u+c. For q in [a,b],
  J2(q)=J2(b)+s log(b/q)+c(1/q-1/b),
  J1(q)=J1(b)+s(b-q)+c log(b/q),
where Jk(q)=integral_q^U phi(u)/u^k du.

For rational r in [1,2], set z=(r-1)/(r+1). Then
  L_N=2 sum_{k=0}^{N-1} z^(2k+1)/(2k+1),
  L_N <= log(r) <= L_N + 2 z^(2N+1)/[(2N+1)(1-z^2)].
The upper bound follows by replacing all remaining denominators by 2N+1 and
summing the geometric series. The script stops when this EXACT rational
remainder is at most 10^-55. Reciprocal and power-of-two reductions handle
other positive arguments.

All interval operations use fractions.Fraction, with lower endpoints rounded
DOWN and upper endpoints rounded UP to integer multiples of 10^-45. This keeps
denominators bounded and encloses every accumulation error. No binary floating
point, Decimal transcendental functions, or unbounded-error quadrature enters
the certificate. Log intervals and all arithmetic are rational enclosures.

Each root bracket has a strictly positive lower enclosure for h' at its left
endpoint and a strictly negative upper enclosure at its right endpoint.
Unimodality proves that the root enclosed is the unique root.

ENDPOINTS

If phi(U-d)~C*d^beta with beta>0, then
  -h'(U-d)~(C/U)*d^beta,
  Psi(U-d)~2*C*d^(beta+2)/[U*(beta+1)*(beta+2)],
  Psi''/Psi~(beta+1)*(beta+2)/d^2.
Strict positivity holds for all q<U after q*. The limit Psi'' at U itself is 0.
For a smooth quadratic endpoint beta=2, the ratio is asymptotic to 12/d^2.
The supplied piecewise-linear interpolants instead have beta=1 exactly on their
last segments; their ratio is asymptotic to 6/d^2. The central normalized slope
coefficient is C=625/124998318 MeV^-2.

For the literal last-positive-node truncation, the central root is instead
in [3.181534857904,3.181534857905]. Then phi has a nonzero left limit at its
cutoff; the vanishing-endpoint premise does not apply. The other two literal
cutoff results are included in certificate.json.
For that discontinuous-cutoff interpretation the proposed recoil correction
has one curvature zero, also enclosed in the JSON. It remains convex above
that zero inside the support, but its value becomes negative before reaching
the cutoff: Psi_full(U-d)~-2*phi(U-)*U*d/M. Thus it is still not a nonnegative
physical rate. This differs from the continuous vanishing-endpoint convention.

THE PROPOSED RECOIL CORRECTION IS NOT CONVEX UP TO U

Define Psi_full exactly as in the question, with LOWER LIMIT q (not the
corrected kinematic lower limit), and put h_full=-Psi_full'/2. Then
  h_full'=h'+[2 J1-3 phi-q phi']/M.
On each linear segment,
  h_full''=-[s*(M+6q)+2c]/(M*q).
The latter has at most one zero on a segment. Checking endpoints and that
possible rational critical point bounds h_full' throughout each segment.
Both one-sided values at knots are checked because phi' can jump there.

For the CONTINUOUS vanishing-endpoint convention the code proves there are
exactly TWO zeros of Psi_full'' in (0,U), with
Psi_full'' positive between them and negative on either side. For the central
column they lie in
  primary: [3.181491880852,3.181491880853] MeV,
  endpoint: [16.357812848766,16.357812848767] MeV.
The primary shift is in [-0.000042977449,-0.000042977447] MeV.
For all three columns its magnitude is <0.000046 MeV (46 eV).
For all three columns, on [q*+0.1,U-0.1],
  -h_full'(q)>5e-9 MeV^-2, at differentiability points and both knot limits.

The failure at the endpoint also has a purely analytic proof. On the final
segment phi(U-d)=C*d, so
  Psi_full(U-d)=-(C*U/M)*d^2+O(d^3),
  Psi_full''(U-)=-2*C*U/M<0.
For a power-law endpoint beta>0 with the additional derivative asymptotic
phi'(U-d)~-C*beta*d^(beta-1), the corresponding leading terms are
-2*C*U*d^(beta+1)/[M*(beta+1)] and -2*C*U*beta*d^(beta-1)/M.
Thus a quadratic physical endpoint does not repair the stated correction.

The nonnegative kernel has its zero at
  u_min(q)=q^2/M+q*sqrt(1+q^2/M^2).
Using this as the lower integration limit gives the kinematically corrected
target, with endpoint q_max=U/sqrt(1+2U/M). That is a different mathematical
function. This certificate does not claim a turning-point enclosure for it.

SCOPE

This is a rigorous computer-assisted statement about the precise uploaded
table and the stated interpolation/normalization conventions. It is not a
certificate for the unprovided Winter evaluation, unprovided +/-1 sigma
spectra, nuclear-response uncertainties, or other interpolations.
